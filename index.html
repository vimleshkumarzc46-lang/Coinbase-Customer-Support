Lock your account

Mobile app:

  1. Access the Coinbase mobile app.
  2. Select the menu, then choose Account & settings.
  3. Select Security, then select Lock Account.
  4. Review the details and confirm that you want to lock your account.

Browser:

  1. Sign in to your Coinbase account from a desktop or mobile browser.
  2. Select your profile menu in the upper corner, then choose Settings.
  3. Select the Security tab.
  4. Under Account Protection, select Lock Account.
  5. Review the details and confirm that you want to lock your account.
